<DocSectionHeader number="05" label="Framework Scope at a Glance" description="The operational v1.1 engine evaluates controls in EDM and DSS. The full framework structure spans additional domains for roadmap evolution." />

| Domain | Focus | Key Controls |
|---|---|---|
| **EDM** | Evaluate, Direct, Monitor | Strategic governance and risk oversight |
| **DSS** | Deliver, Service, Support | Operations, service continuity, incident response |
| **MEA** | Monitor, Evaluate, Assess | Performance measurement and assurance readiness |
